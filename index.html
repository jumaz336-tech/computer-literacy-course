Course Introduction

Computer Literacy is an introductory course designed to provide students with essential knowledge and practical skills needed to use computers effectively in academic, professional, and everyday environments.

The course covers computer hardware and software, operating systems, file management, word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, internet use, email communication, and basic digital skills.
